How to sling

You work the counter directly. Click the food. Drag it where it goes. The money is in the customers' hands.

  1. 1Click the bun stack to lay buns on the plates. Click it again. Three can be out at once.
  2. 2Click the stack of dogs to drop them on the grill. Gold means done. Black means burnt.
  3. 3Drag a grilled dog onto a bun. It snaps. Click ketchup, mustard, relish, or onion to match the bubble.
  4. 4Drag the finished dog to that customer and collect the money. Drag mistakes into the trash.
